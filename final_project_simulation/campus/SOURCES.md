# Geometry provenance

https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The derivative database is layout.json and is offered under ODbL 1.0. It records source way IDs and the local projection/scale. Snapshot downloaded 2026-11-15 from the source URL stored in that file.

The supplied 2026_Printable_Campus_Map_JJ_digital.pdf was used to check landmark names and approximate newer footprints absent from the OSM extract. These are marked per feature. Perspective-derived footprints, heights, and roof details are approximate. Google Maps was not used for asset extraction.

SDFs, OBJ meshes, the plan, and reference image are generated from the derivative database. Attribution is retained here and on the plan.
